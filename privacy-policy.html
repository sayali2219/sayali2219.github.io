Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Artisan Mate (“Artisan Mate”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses and handles personal information when you contact us, submit a project enquiry through a Meta Instant Form, use our website, or communicate with us by phone, WhatsApp or email.

Artisan Mate provides residential interior design, space planning, architecture and related design consultation services. This notice applies to information collected for those enquiries and services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you contact us, we may collect:

  • Contact details such as your name, phone number and, if you choose to provide it, email address.
  • Project information such as your property area or locality, PIN code, the type of work you are considering, property or possession status, expected start timeline and approximate project budget.
  • Any additional details you choose to include in a message, enquiry or conversation.
  • When you use our website, basic technical and interaction information may be collected through cookies or similar technologies, including the pages viewed and interactions with enquiry or contact links.

When you submit a Meta Instant Form, some contact details may be prefilled by Meta based on information in your Meta profile. The information you submit is shared with us so we can respond to your enquiry. Meta also processes information under its own privacy policy.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your interior design or architecture enquiry.
  • Contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about the project you asked us to discuss.
  • Arrange and conduct the free first consultation where requested.
  • Understand your space, requirements, priorities and approximate budget so we can discuss relevant next steps.
  • Maintain enquiry and project records, protect our services, and meet applicable legal or business obligations.
  • Understand website and advertising performance. Our website uses the Meta Pixel for measurement of events such as page views, contact-link interactions and successful website enquiry submissions.

We do not use enquiry details for unrelated promotional messages unless you separately ask for or agree to receive them.

3. Cookies and advertising measurement

Our website uses the Meta Pixel, which may use cookies or similar technologies to help measure website visits and actions and understand the performance of our advertising. Meta may receive event and browser information in connection with these tools and process it under its own terms and privacy policy. You can review Meta’s privacy information at Meta’s Privacy Policy and manage relevant preferences through your Meta account and browser settings.

4. When information may be shared

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information only as reasonably needed to handle your enquiry or operate our services, including:

  • With Meta when you submit a form on Facebook or Instagram. Meta’s handling of information on its platforms is also governed by Meta’s own policies.
  • With service providers that help operate our website, receive website form submissions, communicate with you, maintain records or measure advertising performance.
  • Where disclosure is required by applicable law or is reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users or services.

5. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and project information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, manage the discussion or any resulting project, maintain appropriate business records, and meet applicable legal obligations. When information is no longer required for these purposes, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or otherwise remove it from active use, subject to any lawful retention requirements.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of information you have provided, or to ask us to stop further follow-up about your enquiry. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and may retain information where required for legitimate or legal record-keeping purposes.

7. Keeping information secure

We take reasonable steps to protect enquiry information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No method of online transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-party platforms and links

Our forms, website tools and communication channels may involve third-party platforms, including Meta, WhatsApp and providers used to operate website enquiry submissions. When you use those services, their own privacy notices and terms may also apply. We encourage you to review them.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our information practices or services change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
