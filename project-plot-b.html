01 / ARCHITECTURE · RESIDENTIAL

Plot B

A visual reading of a contemporary residential tower from the supplied architectural references.

PROJECT READING

Vertical rhythm with a controlled facade language.

The supplied views show a tall residential building organised around a strong vertical axis, repeated glazed openings and projecting frames. Deep reveals and narrow vertical fins create shadow lines that break down the height of the facade.

The surrounding landscape and road edge are treated as part of the composition, keeping the building connected to a planted setting rather than reading as an isolated object.

DESIGN HIGHLIGHTS

Material, opening and proportion.

01

Facade

Cool neutral massing with repeated horizontal and vertical articulation.

02

Openings

Large glazed openings and recessed balconies create a consistent rhythm across the elevation.

03

Landscape

Green edges and planted surroundings soften the hard architectural geometry.

04

Light

Projected frames and fins create changing shadow depth through the day.
