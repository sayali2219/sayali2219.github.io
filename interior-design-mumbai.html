A DESIGN-LED APPROACH

Know what you are deciding before work begins.

Interior design involves more than selecting finishes. Layout, storage, lighting, circulation, furniture and materials affect how a home works every day. We bring these decisions into one design conversation so the project has a clearer direction before execution starts.

WHAT THE DESIGN CAN COVER

A plan for the details that matter.

The exact deliverables are confirmed in your project brief and proposal, so you know what is included before the design work begins.

01

Space planning

Room layouts, circulation, furniture placement and storage planned around your household's routines and the measurements available.

02

Kitchen, bedroom & storage

Design direction for kitchens, wardrobes, utility areas and built-in storage, with attention to access and everyday use.

03

Materials & finishes

Guidance on colour, surface finishes, textures and the way selected materials work together across the home.

04

Lighting & electrical planning

Consider lighting layers, task areas, switch locations and electrical requirements as part of the wider room plan.

05

2D layouts & 3D visualisation

Visual design material to help you understand the proposed layout, proportions and overall direction before execution.

06

Scope & budget discussion

Clarify priorities, inclusions and exclusions. Any early cost discussion is indicative until the final scope, site conditions and contractor quotations are confirmed.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

What does an interior design package include?

It depends on the project brief. The agreed proposal can cover space planning, 2D layouts, 3D visualisations, material and finish direction, lighting considerations and design handover. The exact deliverables and revision rounds are listed before the work begins.

Does Artisan Mate provide turnkey interior execution?

Artisan Mate's core offer is design and consultation. You can appoint your own contractor to execute the agreed design. Any additional coordination or site guidance is discussed separately and included only when confirmed in the written proposal.

Can you help plan interiors around my budget?

Yes. Share your budget range and priorities at the beginning. We can use them to guide design choices and discuss an indicative project range. Final execution pricing depends on the approved scope, site conditions, material selections and contractor quotations.

Which homes and rooms can you help with?

We consider whole-home interiors, apartment layouts, kitchens, bedrooms, wardrobes, bathrooms, utility areas and individual-room redesigns. The right scope depends on the brief and site information.

What should I share before the first consultation?

If available, share your floor plan, approximate area, site photographs, project location, preferred timeline, budget range and a short list of what you want to improve. You can begin with whatever information you have.

Where is Artisan Mate based?

Artisan Mate is based in Mumbai. Share your project location when enquiring so availability and the best consultation format can be discussed.

How much does interior design cost?

Fees depend on the size of the space, project complexity and the design documents you need. Artisan Mate confirms the scope, deliverables and fee in a proposal before paid design work begins.
