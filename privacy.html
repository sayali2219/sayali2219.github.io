Privacy Notice

Last updated: 1 November 2026. This notice explains how information submitted through the Artisan Mate website is used.

Information you submit

When you use the project enquiry form, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, project location, property type, approximate budget, preferred timeline, requested service and any information you include in your message.

How the information is used

Artisan Mate uses enquiry information to respond to your request, understand the project brief, arrange a consultation and communicate about a potential design engagement. Please do not include payment-card information, government identification numbers or other sensitive information in the enquiry message.

Service providers and contact links

The website enquiry form is submitted through FormSubmit and routed to Artisan Mate's contact email. FormSubmit processes the submission as an external service and may process technical information needed to provide that service. When you choose to open WhatsApp or another external link, that service's own privacy terms apply.

Website measurement

The homepage currently loads Meta Pixel for page-view and contact/lead measurement. Meta may use cookies or similar technologies and receive technical or interaction information in accordance with its own terms and your platform settings. This website does not currently provide an on-page cookie preference manager.

Access, correction or deletion requests

To ask a question about an enquiry you submitted, or to request correction or deletion of information held in the enquiry correspondence, email kudvesayali.sk@gmail.com. Some information may remain in service-provider systems or business records where required for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

External websites

Links to WhatsApp, social platforms and other websites are provided for convenience. Artisan Mate does not control the privacy practices of external services.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the website, enquiry process or tools change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

This notice is an operational website notice, not a legal opinion. It should be reviewed against the business's actual data-handling practices and applicable law before relying on it as a complete compliance document.
